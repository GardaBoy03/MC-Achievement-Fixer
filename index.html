Bedrock Edition • level.dat Tool

👋 Hi! This tool helps re-enable World Achievements on your Minecraft Bedrock world where they've been disabled.

Achievements are usually disabled because one of these has happened in your world at some point:

  • It was opened in Creative mode
  • Cheats/Allow Cheats was turned on
  • Any Experimental Gameplay toggle was enabled
  • The world still uses a behavior pack / resource pack (add-on)

This "disabled" status stays saved in the world file even after all the options above are manually turned off/removed in-game.

⚠️ Always back up your world before processing. This tool changes several flags in level.dat and can remove behavior/resource packs from the world.

If your world depends on custom blocks/items from an add-on, or was built using Experimental Gameplay, that content may turn into "unknown" or disappear once the pack is removed. Achievements will become active, but check the world carefully before relying on it permanently.
